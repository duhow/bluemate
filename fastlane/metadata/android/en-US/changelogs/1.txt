Initial release.

- BLE iBeacon advertising and scanning to find nearby Bluemate users
- Distance estimation in meters from RSSI signal strength
- Compass orientation display using device sensors
- Foreground service for continuous scanning in the background
- Battery optimization exemption request
- Fixed button being unreachable on fullscreen/edge-to-edge devices
- Fixed two devices not being able to discover each other
- Compass heading now updates smoothly every 0.2 seconds
- Improved Android 13+ nearby discovery reliability by fixing BLE scan matching
- Paired Bluetooth devices now stay visible in the list, even when temporarily out of range
- Added a discovery mode selector so you can run scan mode or beacon-only mode for better cross-device detection
- Fixed nearby device discovery by requesting required location permissions automatically
- Paired and unavailable devices are now hidden from the scan view; only visible Bluemates are shown
- Devices that have not responded in over 15 seconds are shown in grey
- A rotating compass arrow now shows your current heading on the main screen
- Beacon now broadcasts every second and includes the compass heading, so nearby users can see your direction arrow
- Tap a Bluemate in the device list to send a call signal that relays across nearby devices until it reaches the target
- The target device plays the phone ringtone (using the alarm channel to bypass Do Not Disturb) and the audio stops when the app is opened
- The target device automatically sends an acknowledgement back so the caller can see the call was received
- Compass heading is now tracked by the background service, so it stays up to date even when the app is in the background or the screen is off
- Fixed duplicate ringtone and repeated ACK when the same call ping arrived via multiple relay hops
- Fixed repeated "call received" toasts: each unique call is now acknowledged only once
- Incoming calls now show a heads-up notification that appears on the lock screen and wakes the device
- Tap the notification or dismiss it directly from the notification
- Fixed BLE call reception when the screen is locked or in power save mode
